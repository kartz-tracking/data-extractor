Extract Data

A Google Sheets add-on that reads a screen recording into the spreadsheet it is installed in.

You film the in-game ranking list, open Extensions → Extract Data, and drop the recording into the window that appears over the sheet. It reads the names and scores, matches them against your roster tab, shows you what it found, and inserts the rows you keep — without touching a formula, a colour or a dropdown that was already there.

What it can reach

  • The spreadsheet it is installed in, and no other file. That is the spreadsheets.currentonly permission — not your Drive, not your other sheets.
  • Your email address (openid), which it uses to prove to its own server that the request came from someone this spreadsheet is shared with.
  • Permission to show a window inside Google Sheets.

Where the recording goes

It is read in your browser. The video is never uploaded anywhere. Still frames sampled from it are sent to a model provider to be read, and nothing is kept afterwards.

Who can use it

Whoever the spreadsheet is shared with. There are no accounts, no passwords and no list to join — Google's own sharing settings decide, and rows can only be written by people who can already edit the file.
